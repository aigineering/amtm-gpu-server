Scenarios

ScenarioMeaning
soloModel alone on the GPU (other instances stopped) — per-model best case. ShareGPT workload (realistic short chat).
colocatedAll models serving, load driven against every endpoint simultaneously — the production topology. ShareGPT workload. SLO verdicts are judged here.
contextSolo capacity probe: random long inputs at ~80% of the model's context window, zero prefix reuse (worst-case KV pressure).
contextpairSame long-input probe against all endpoints at once — "does this context + all models fit, at how many users".

ColumnMeaning
usersConcurrent simulated users (per model) for that row

ITL p95Inter-token latency, ms — streaming smoothness; ≤100ms ≈ ≥10 tok/s per user, comfortably above reading speed
TPOT p95Time per output token, ms — close cousin of ITL, includes scheduling effects

GPU hitGPU prefix-cache hit rate during that row (Δhits/Δqueries vs the previous snapshot in the run)
ext hitExternal (RAM-offloaded) prefix-cache hit rate during that row — requires KV offloading
SLOPASS/FAIL against the working targets (docs/benchmarking.md): TTFT p95 ≤ 1.5s (≤2.5s at 50 users) AND ITL p95 ≤ 100ms. Evaluated on every row; read per scenario — colocated is the binding product judgment, solo is a model's standalone ceiling, and context/contextpair failing at high tiers is the expected capacity cliff (worst-case probe), not a defect
